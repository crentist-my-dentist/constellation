## Current goal
Turn three drifting friendships into regular ones by having a standing rhythm with
each, instead of waiting for it to happen.

## Where I am (honest)
I think about these people warmly but I initiate maybe once a quarter, usually in
response to them. Two of the three I haven't seen in person this year.

## The gap
I treat reaching out as something that needs an occasion. It doesn't — the barrier is
me overthinking whether I'm bothering them, plus no default plan so it never gets
scheduled.

## Next moves
- Text the two I haven't seen and propose an actual date, not "we should hang out."
- Set one recurring thing (monthly walk / coffee) with at least one of them.
- Notice the "am I bothering them?" thought and send anyway; log how it actually goes.
