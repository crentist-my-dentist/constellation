# North star — friendship

## What "great" looks like here
A small handful of friendships that are genuinely two-way and durable — people I see
in person regularly, who I can be honest with and who can be honest with me. Not a big
network; a few deep ones that survive busy seasons because we both tend them.

## Why it matters to me
Left alone I default to work and let friendships coast until they quietly fade. I
don't want to look up in ten years with no one who knew me along the way. Closeness is
worth deliberate effort.

## What I refuse to trade for it
I won't fake interest or keep people around out of guilt. Depth over quantity, and
honesty over smoothing things over.
