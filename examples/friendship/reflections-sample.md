# Reflections — sample (candid)

## 2026-08-29
Reached out to Sam after months. Kept almost not sending it — the old "they're
probably busy, I'll be a bother" story. Sent it anyway. They replied in ten minutes,
clearly glad to hear from me.

The honest read: the distance was never them cooling on me, it was me pre-rejecting on
their behalf and calling it politeness. That's the pattern to watch — I do the same
thing at work when I go quiet instead of asking. Same root: assuming I'm a burden.

**A fact from this** (would go to the journal / wins): reconnected with Sam, planning
something monthly. **The candid part** (stays here): the reason I'd drifted was my own
avoidance, not their disinterest.
