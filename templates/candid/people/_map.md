# People map (candid — local only)

Honest reads on the people in your life across all areas: who they are to you, what
they want, how they affect you, where the friction or the support is. This is for
*your* clarity — kept private, never shared, never pushed to a remote.

Be fair, not cynical. The goal is to understand people accurately so you can navigate
well and treat them well — not to keep a grudge ledger.
